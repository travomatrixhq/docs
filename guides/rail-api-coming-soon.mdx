## High-Speed Rail Integration

We are bringing European and Indian rail content to the Travomatrix platform. 

### What to Expect
- **Direct NDC Links:** Integration with major rail operators.
- **Seat Selection:** Interactive coach maps for rail bookings.
- **Ancillaries:** Meals and lounge access integration.

The Rail API will follow the same idempotent booking lifecycle as our Flight API.
