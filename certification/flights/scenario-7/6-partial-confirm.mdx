---
title: "Step 6: Confirm Split"
openapi: "POST /flight/booking/partial-cancel/confirm"
---

Confirm the cancellation. The B2B system abstracts the provider integration:
* **GDS Carriers (Amadeus/Travelport)**: The system handles `DivideReservation` logic to split the PNR.
* **NDC/LCC Carriers**: The system handles direct segment removal.

### Request Setup
<ParamField header="Idempotency-Key" type="string" required>Generate a random string.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="booking_id" type="string" required>Paste the `booking_id`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="passengers_to_cancel" type="array" required>Array of passenger IDs (e.g., `["ADT2"]`). Pass what you passed in the quote.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="segments_to_cancel" type="array" required>Array of segment indices. Pass what you passed in the quote.</ParamField>

### Instructions
1. Hit **Send** to trigger the background atomic wallet debit and provider cancellation.
2. The remaining passengers stay in the original `TICKETED` PNR, and you will receive a partial refund.

<Warning>
  **Requirement:** Review the response payload carefully.
</Warning>
