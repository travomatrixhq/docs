title: "Step 2: Confirm Split"
openapi: "POST /flight/booking/partial-cancel/confirm"
---

Confirm the cancellation. The system will handle the PNR Splitting logic with the airline, leaving the remaining passengers in a `TICKETED` state.

### Request Setup
<ParamField header="Idempotency-Key" type="string" required>Generate a random string.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="booking_id" type="string" required>Paste the `booking_id`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="passengers_to_cancel" type="array" required>Array of passenger IDs (e.g., ["ADT2"]).</ParamField>

### Instructions
1. Review the requirements and set up the request payload.
2. Hit the **Send** button.

<Warning>
  **Requirement:** Review the response payload carefully.
</Warning>
