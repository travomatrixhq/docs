title: "Step 6: Reschedule Quote"
openapi: "POST /flight/booking/reschedule/quote"
---

Provide your `booking_id` and the `new_offer_id`. The system calculates the `fare_difference_paise` and any `change_penalty_paise`.

### Request Setup
<ParamField body="booking_id" type="string" required>Paste the `booking_id`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="new_offer_id" type="string" required>Paste the `offer_id` from the new flight search.</ParamField>

### Instructions
1. Review the requirements and set up the request payload.
2. Hit the **Send** button.

<Warning>
  **Requirement:** Review the response payload carefully.
</Warning>
