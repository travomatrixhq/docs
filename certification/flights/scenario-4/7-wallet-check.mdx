title: "Step 3: Verify Refund"
openapi: "GET /flight/agency/wallet/transactions"

Retrieve your transaction ledger using your `agent_id`. You should see a new ledger entry with `"type": "REFUND"`.

### Request Setup
<ParamField query="agent_id" type="string" required>Your UAT Agent ID.</ParamField>

### Instructions
1. Review the requirements and set up the request payload.
2. Hit the **Send** button.

<Warning>
  **Requirement:** Review the ledger to confirm the transaction.
</Warning>
